How to play

  1. Sail three acts and kill each act boss — the Sky-Tyrant Vex waits at the end. Death ends the run.
  2. Each turn you gain 3 energy and draw 4 cards. Click a card, then a foe if it asks. End your turn when spent.
  3. Read intents. ATK shows incoming damage; DEFEND, EMPOWER and HEX telegraph the rest.
  4. Play the triangle: Might beats Guile beats Focus beats Might (x1.5 / x0.75). Mind the front row — it shields the back.
  5. Chart your course across fights, elites, rest stops, shops, taverns and events. Draft 1 of 3 cards after victories, 1 of 3 boons each level.
  6. Spend wisely: gold for shops and taverns, dust to strike basics from your deck, shards for relic trades.
